Research

I develop algorithms and software for detecting and reconstructing extrachromosomal DNA (ecDNA) and other focal amplifications in cancer genomes. ecDNA carries oncogenes on circles outside the chromosomes, is inherited unevenly at cell division, and is associated with drug resistance and poor outcomes. Our methods recover these structures from short-read, long-read and optical-mapping data.

I work with Vineet Bafna, founded and lead the AmpliconSuite project, and lead bioinformatics for the eDyNAmiC Cancer Grand Challenges team. I am also part of the ARPA-H ADAPT program and the Moores Cancer Center Molecular Tumor Board. PhD, Bioinformatics and Systems Biology, UC San Diego, 2021.

Software

  • AmpliconSuite-pipeline: end-to-end focal amplification analysis from WGS.
  • AmpliconClassifier: classification of ecDNA, BFB and other amplicons.
  • AmpliconRepository.org: a community database of ecDNA annotations (project lead).
  • AmpliconArchitect (maintainer), AmpliconReconstructor, CoRAL (co-developer), CycleViz, FaNDOM, ecSimulator.

These tools have been used to analyse 50,626 samples in 55 published studies and preprints, 39 of them by other groups.
