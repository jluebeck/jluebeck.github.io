AboutLa Jolla, CA

I develop algorithms and software for detecting and reconstructing extrachromosomal DNA (ecDNA) and other oncogene amplifications in cancer genomes.

ecDNA carries oncogenes on circles outside the chromosomes, is inherited unevenly when cells divide, and is linked to drug resistance and poor outcomes. Finding it in sequencing data is a graph reconstruction problem. I work in Vineet Bafna's group at UC San Diego and lead bioinformatics for the eDyNAmiC Cancer Grand Challenges team.
