Research

Detecting and reconstructing extrachromosomal DNA in cancer genomes.

Cancers often amplify oncogenes on circles of DNA outside the chromosomes. These circles, ecDNA, have no centromere and are split unevenly at each cell division, which lets tumours change oncogene dose quickly. ecDNA is linked to drug resistance and poor outcomes. I develop graph-based methods that reconstruct these structures from short-read, long-read and optical-mapping data, and I apply them in large collaborative cancer studies.
