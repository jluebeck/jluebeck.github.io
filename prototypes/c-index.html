AboutLa Jolla, CA

I develop algorithms and software for detecting and reconstructing extrachromosomal DNA (ecDNA) and other oncogene amplifications in cancer genomes.

I work in Vineet Bafna's group at UC San Diego and lead bioinformatics for the eDyNAmiC Cancer Grand Challenges team. Before this I did my PhD in Bioinformatics & Systems Biology at UCSD and consulted for Boundless Bio.

Why ecDNA

A common driver

About 17% of tumours carry oncogenes on circular DNA outside the chromosomes. Patients whose tumours carry it tend to do worse.

A moving target

ecDNA is passed on unevenly at each cell division, so tumours can change oncogene dose quickly and adapt to treatment.

A reconstruction problem

Finding a circle in short sequencing reads means building and searching a breakpoint graph. That is the part I work on.
